# CampusX Placement & Internship Regulations

## Article 1: Eligibility and Registration
1.1 Students registering for campus recruitment drives must have a minimum CGPA of 6.0 with zero active arrears at the time of registration.
1.2 Individual recruiting companies may specify higher minimum CGPA thresholds (typically 7.0 to 8.5) and discipline-specific department restrictions.
1.3 Minimum overall institutional attendance of 80% is mandatory to participate in campus recruitment.

## Article 2: Offer Acceptance Policy (One-Student-One-Job Rule)
2.1 To ensure equitable career opportunities across the graduating cohort, CampusX enforces a strict "One Student, One Regular Job Offer" policy.
2.2 Once a student accepts an on-campus placement offer, they are automatically ineligible for subsequent drives, with the following single exception:
- **Dream / Super Dream Upgrades**: A student holding a standard offer (CTC < ₹10 LPA) is eligible to apply for Dream companies offering CTC >= 1.5x of their current offer, or Super Dream companies offering CTC >= ₹20 LPA.
2.3 Any student reneging on an accepted corporate offer is immediately blacklisted from campus placement services.

## Article 3: Semester Internships & NOC Procedures
3.1 Final-year (Semester 8) students are permitted to pursue full-time industry internships for 4 to 6 months upon securing an institutional No Objection Certificate (NOC).
3.2 NOC applications must include the official employer offer letter, compensation details (minimum stipend ₹15,000/month), and designated corporate mentor details.
3.3 Weekly progress reports and a final viva voce examination are mandatory to receive academic internship credits.
