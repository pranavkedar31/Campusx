# CampusX University Examination & Assessment Policy

## Article 1: Evaluation Split & Internal Continuous Assessment
1.1 Total course grade evaluation is partitioned into:
- Continuous Internal Assessment (CIA): 40% weightage (Midterm exam 20%, Quizzes/Assignments 10%, Lab practicals/Term project 10%).
- End-Semester University Examination: 60% weightage.
1.2 To pass a course, a student must secure at least 40% marks in the End-Semester Examination and an aggregate score of 40% across both CIA and End-Semester combined.

## Article 2: Hall Ticket and Exam Eligibility
2.1 Digital Hall Tickets are generated 7 days prior to examination commencement via the CampusX portal.
2.2 Hall ticket issuance is strictly conditional upon:
- Minimum overall attendance of 75% in each enrolled subject.
- Full clearance of university tuition and laboratory fee dues.
- No pending disciplinary suspension orders from the Proctorial Board.

## Article 3: Revaluation and Supplementary Examinations
3.1 Students may apply for answer script photocopying and revaluation within 10 calendar days of grade publication.
3.2 A flat fee of ₹500 per subject applies for revaluation, refundable if the grade increases by more than 10%.
3.3 Supplementary (Arrear) examinations are scheduled immediately following semester break periods.
3.4 Any form of exam malpractice or mobile phone possession in the exam hall incurs immediate paper cancellation and a one-year examination debarment.
