# CampusX University Academic Regulations & Credit Framework

## Article 1: Credit Structure and Degree Requirements
1.1 An undergraduate B.Tech degree requires successful completion of a minimum of 160 academic credits over 8 regular semesters.
1.2 Maximum duration permitted to complete the degree program is 6 academic years (12 semesters) from the date of initial matriculation.
1.3 Each lecture hour per week corresponds to 1 credit; each practical laboratory session of 2 hours per week corresponds to 1 credit.

## Article 2: Grading Scale and CGPA Computation
2.1 Letter grades are awarded on a 10-point scale:
- O (Outstanding): Grade Point 10.0 (Marks >= 90%)
- A+ (Excellent): Grade Point 9.0 (Marks 80-89%)
- A (Very Good): Grade Point 8.0 (Marks 70-79%)
- B+ (Good): Grade Point 7.0 (Marks 60-69%)
- B (Above Average): Grade Point 6.0 (Marks 50-59%)
- C (Pass): Grade Point 5.0 (Marks 40-49%)
- F (Fail): Grade Point 0.0 (Marks < 40%)
2.2 Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) is calculated as the weighted average of Grade Points obtained across all registered courses, weighted by their respective course credits.

## Article 3: Academic Standing, Probation, and Detention
3.1 A student must maintain a minimum CGPA of 5.0 to remain in good academic standing.
3.2 A student whose CGPA drops below 5.0 at the conclusion of any academic year will be placed on Academic Probation for the subsequent semester.
3.3 If a student fails to elevate their CGPA to 5.0 by the end of the probationary period, they will be subject to Academic Year Detention until prerequisite backlogs are cleared.
3.4 Students with more than 4 uncleared active arrears (backlogs) are not permitted to register for higher-year core engineering subjects.
