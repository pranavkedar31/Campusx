# CampusX University Campus Life, Hostel & Conduct Code

## Article 1: General Code of Discipline & Anti-Ragging Policy
1.1 CampusX enforces a Zero Tolerance Policy toward ragging, harassment, discrimination, or intimidation of any nature.
1.2 Engaging in or abetting ragging is a non-bailable offense under national statutory regulations and results in immediate permanent expulsion from the university and filing of an official police report.
1.3 Anti-Ragging helpline numbers and anonymous reporting portals are accessible 24/7 on the CampusX mobile app and website.

## Article 2: Residence Halls (Hostel) Regulations & Curfew
2.1 Residential entry gates close strictly at 9:30 PM on weekdays and 10:30 PM on weekends and public holidays.
2.2 Students desiring night-out passes or weekend home visits must submit digital parent-consented leave requests via the portal at least 24 hours prior.
2.3 Possession or consumption of alcoholic beverages, narcotics, or prohibited substances within university premises is strictly prohibited and leads to immediate rustication.

## Article 3: Library and Computing Facilities
3.1 The central library and digital research lab remain open 24/7 during midterm and final examination schedules.
3.2 Institutional computing resources and campus Wi-Fi networks are monitored for cybersecurity compliance; downloading unauthorized copyrighted material or running commercial cryptocurrency mining will result in network credential revocation.
