# CampusX University Attendance Compliance & Leave Rules

## Article 1: Mandatory Attendance Thresholds
1.1 Students are expected to maintain 100% attendance in all scheduled lectures, tutorials, and practical laboratory sessions.
1.2 The minimum statutory attendance required to sit for the end-semester final examinations is 75% per course.
1.3 Students with attendance between 65.0% and 74.9% may apply for Condonation of Attendance on valid medical grounds or university-authorized representation, subject to Dean of Academic Affairs approval and payment of a condonation fee.
1.4 Students whose attendance drops below 65.0% are strictly debarred from appearing in the semester examination for that course and must re-register the subject as a repeat course.

## Article 2: On-Duty (OD) Leave & Extracurricular Representation
2.1 On-Duty (OD) attendance credit is granted to students representing the university in sanctioned hackathons, academic conferences, inter-collegiate sports, and NCC/NSS camps.
2.2 OD requests must be submitted online at least 3 business days in advance with faculty mentor recommendation.
2.3 Maximum OD allocation is capped at 10% of total class sessions per semester.

## Article 3: Medical Leave Protocol
3.1 Medical leave exceeding 3 consecutive days requires submission of a certified medical certificate from a registered physician within 5 days of returning to campus.
3.2 Chronic medical conditions requiring hospitalization must be formally registered with the University Health Center.
