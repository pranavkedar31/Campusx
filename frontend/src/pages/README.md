# Frontend Pages Directory
Views corresponding to application routes will be structured here starting from Phase 2:
- Landing / Public Portal (`/`)
- Authentication (`/login`)
- Role Dashboards (`/dashboard`)
- Student Profiles & Management (`/students`, `/students/:id`)
- Academic Management (`/academics`, `/attendance`, `/performance`)
- Predictive Risk & Early Warning (`/risk-analysis`)
- Notices & Campus Events (`/notices`, `/events`)
- Digital Certificates (`/certificates`)
- Placement Intelligence (`/placements`, `/career-intelligence`)
- AI Assistants (`/ai-assistant`, `/campus-assistant`)
- Institutional Analytics (`/analytics`, `/documents`)
- Administration (`/users`, `/audit-logs`, `/settings`, `/profile`)
